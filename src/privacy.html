Privacy

This personal website uses PostHog Cloud EU to understand which pages people visit, how readers engage with articles, and which links they choose to follow. Articles and assets remain static files; there are no contact forms or advertising trackers.

Analytics runs without cookies or identifiers stored in your browser. PostHog processes the IP address and browser information to generate a temporary identifier on its servers that changes daily. This does not reliably identify a returning reader across different days or devices.

Events include page views, an article engagement signal, and clicks on email links, social profiles, RSS and downloadable files. An email click does not tell this site whether you sent a message. Analytics does not collect email addresses, message contents, copied text, or session recordings. Page URLs are cleaned; only a limited set of campaign labels is retained.

Analytics is disabled when your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, and on development and preview hosts. You can also add ?analytics=off to a page URL to disable collection on that page visit; this setting is not saved between pages. Browser blocking tools may prevent collection as well.

PostHog receives this analytics data through its EU service. See PostHog’s privacy policy and cookieless tracking documentation. Hosting infrastructure may process request data to deliver and protect the site.

The theme follows your browser or operating system settings. This site does not save a theme preference. The public MCP endpoint reads the same published profile and articles; it has no account or editing access.
